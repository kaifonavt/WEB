Peter Parker is a young New Yorker trying to balance everyday life with the responsibility of being a hero. Behind the mask, he faces familiar struggles with studying, work, and relationships.

Peter could use his abilities only for himself. Instead, he chooses to help others, even when doing the right thing comes at a personal cost.
